Data Deletion Instructions

CRDC CRM - Messenger Test
Effective date: October 13, 2026

This test integration is operated by an individual reachable at zedyhans@gmail.com. Deletion requests are handled manually.

Request deletion

  1. Email zedyhans@gmail.com with the subject "Messenger test data deletion request".
  2. Identify the Facebook Page you messaged and give the approximate date of the conversation and your Messenger display name so the operator can locate it. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary sensitive information.
  3. The operator may ask you to confirm the request through the same Messenger account before deleting data, to protect against unauthorized requests.
  4. After verification, the operator will manually delete the associated local test conversation, messages, and stored sender-profile data, remove identifiable local test logs where available, and confirm completion by email.

Limits of deletion

This process removes data controlled by the operator in the local test integration. It does not delete messages or records held by Meta, the Facebook Page's Business Suite inbox, or other providers. Manage those copies through the corresponding provider's controls and policies.

Sending another message may create a new test record. Tell the operator if you also want the integration to stop retaining future messages from your account.
